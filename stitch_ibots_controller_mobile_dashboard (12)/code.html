Car Controller

Standard

Omnidirectional drive, cutoff switch & dance mode

expand_more

The Car Controller view dispatches instant single-character UART commands over serial stream for H-Bridge motor drivers (L298N, TB6612FNG, or ESP32 Motor Shields).

swap_calls Movement Commands
F = Drive Forward
B = Reverse / Back
L = Pivot Left
R = Pivot Right
cancel Emergency Brake
S = Instant Motor Cutoff

Releasing buttons also automatically issues 'S' stop safety pulses.

Special Triggers
  • volume_up Horn: Toggles onboard active buzzer / piezo pin.
  • highlight Headlights: Toggles ultra-bright forward LED array.
  • animation Dance Mode: Runs an automated sequence of spins, reverse wiggles, and alternating flashing lights.
Hardware Note: If wheels do not spin, ensure 9V/12V motor battery is turned ON and ground (GND) is shared with the ESP32.

Robotic Arm Control

Multi-Axis

Servo angle adjustment (0°–180°), multi-joint expansion & centering

expand_more

The Robotic Arm module controls articulated servos across individual joint axes (Base A, Shoulder B, Elbow C, and auxiliary joints).

Standard Controls
  • linear_scale Servo Sliders (0°–180°): Continuous angle manipulation with real-time degree readout.
  • add_circle + Add Movement: Dynamically append extra axes (D, E, F...) for 4-DOF, 5-DOF, or 6-DOF robotic arms.
  • adjust Center All: Instantly resets all connected servos to 90° neutral position.
  • remove_circle Remove Axis: Delete custom auxiliary axes while preserving core A, B, and C joints.
code Serial Protocol

Dispatches commands formatted as [Axis][Angle] (e.g. A90, B120, C45).

Hardware Note: Ensure external 5V/6V high-current servo power supply is used to prevent ESP32 brownouts during multi-servo torque spikes.

Troubleshooting

Diagnostics

Connection failures, motor power & sensor fixes

expand_more
bluetooth_disabled Device fails to connect or discover

Ensure your ESP32 is powered via reliable 5V USB (not sagging battery). Verify smartphone Bluetooth permissions are granted and cycle the phone's Bluetooth toggle.

videocam_off Camera stream is blank or sluggish

Check camera ribbon cable seated firmly in FPC connector. Lower stream resolution from High to Medium/Low in Settings to preserve 2.4GHz bandwidth.

electric_meter Commands sent but motors don't spin

Ensure the motor driver ground (GND) is wired together with ESP32 ground pin. Without a shared common reference ground, logic signals cannot be interpreted.
